About me

Se Sopin is an international yoga teacher and Inside Flow specialist. On a mission to integrate the transformative tools of yoga into everyday life, Se hosts global workshops, masterclasses, and vibrant online sessions.

Se provides high-quality practices on yoga, movement, and mindfulness—inspiring practitioners of all ages, shapes, and experience levels to embrace self-love and presence.
